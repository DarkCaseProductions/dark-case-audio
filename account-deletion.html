Request Account Deletion

Dark Case Audio

You can request deletion of your Dark Case Audio member account and associated personal data without using the app. This page explains how to make a request and what happens to your information.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to darkcaseaudios@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Dark Case Audio Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the email address registered to your Dark Case Audio account.
  4. Include your member username and state clearly that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

Email an account deletion request

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before carrying out the request. We will process verified requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is permitted by law or is reasonably necessary to resolve a complex request. If more time is needed, we will explain why.

Data that will be deleted

After verifying your request, we will delete or anonymise the account information associated with your membership, where technically possible and subject to the retention exceptions below. This includes your member account and sign-in record, profile information such as your username and email address, and personal data held for ordinary membership and access to member features.

Information that may be retained

Some information may need to be kept for a limited period where required by law, for tax or accounting records, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Where this applies, we restrict the information to what is necessary, retain it only for the applicable legal or operational retention period, and securely delete it when it is no longer needed.

  • Purchase and transaction records may be retained for the period required by applicable tax and accounting laws.
  • Copies of private messages already delivered to other members may remain in those members' accounts or conversation views; deleting your account does not necessarily remove copies already received by someone else.
  • Information held independently by payment or other service providers is subject to their own retention policies. You may need to contact the relevant provider about data they hold independently.
  • Encrypted backups and security logs may take a limited period to expire through normal rotation, and are not used for ordinary account access.

Deleting your account does not automatically

  • Cancel a separate payment arrangement or refund a completed purchase.
  • Remove information that another member has independently received or saved.
  • Delete records that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Request deletion of other data without closing your account

If you want to ask us to delete particular personal information but keep your account, email darkcaseaudios@gmail.com with the subject Dark Case Audio Data Deletion Request. Explain which information you want deleted. We will assess the request under applicable data protection law.
