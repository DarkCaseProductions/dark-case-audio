The Strangest Crimes — Bizarre British Cases That Left Investigators Baffled

Step inside twenty extraordinary British crime cases involving hospital poisonings, hidden bodies, unidentified victims, missing people, wrongful convictions and investigations that refused to stay closed. From shocking discoveries to decades-old mysteries, these gripping stories reveal the evidence, the mistakes and the unanswered questions behind some of Britain’s most disturbing crimes. Presented by Lynsey Willetts, this Dark Case Audio production explores the cases where the truth proved stranger than anyone expected.
