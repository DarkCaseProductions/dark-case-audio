How to use this pack

Record what each source actually establishes before interpreting it. Keep confirmed facts, reported accounts, interpretations and unknowns separate. In crime investigations, do not accuse anyone beyond established court findings. In paranormal and cryptid investigations, an unexplained report is not proof of a supernatural event or unknown species.

Confidence labels: Confirmed — supported by an official record, court finding or primary source. Reported — attributed to a witness or named source. Interpretation — an explanation drawn from available material. Unverified — reliable supporting evidence has not been located. Unknown — the available material does not answer the question.
