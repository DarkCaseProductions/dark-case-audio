The Austin Yogurt Shop Murders

Austin, Texas, USA · 1991–2026

Individual investigator pack

Use this working file to examine the documented record, test explanations and record your own findings.

Case briefing

Four teenage girls were murdered inside the I Can’t Believe It’s Yogurt! shop in Austin in December 1991. Years of arrests, convictions and overturned cases followed. The website case material records a 2025 police identification of deceased Robert Eugene Brashers as the suspect and a 2026 judicial declaration that the four men previously accused were innocent. Brashers died in 1999 and was never tried for these murders.

Editorial and evidence safeguards

Distinguish police investigative conclusions from court findings. Brashers was identified by police but was never tried or convicted in this case. Do not describe his guilt as a judicial finding. The four men previously accused were formally declared innocent in 2026.

Case-specific investigation questions

1. What physical and forensic evidence was recovered from the shop, and how did fire and water affect its preservation?
2. Why did the original investigation focus on Pierce, Springsteen, Scott and Welborn, and what ultimately happened to each case?
3. What did the DNA profile exclude, and what did the later Y-STR comparison establish about Brashers?
4. How should the NIBIN association, firearm serial-number link and border-stop evidence be weighed alongside the DNA evidence?
5. What did the 2026 judicial exoneration establish, and how is it different from a criminal conviction of the person police identified?
6. Which aspects of the investigation remain open, and what further testing or records could clarify them?
