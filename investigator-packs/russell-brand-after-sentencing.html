Russell Brand: The Case After Sentencing

United Kingdom · Developments recorded through 24 November 2026

Individual investigator pack

Use this working file to examine the documented record, test explanations and record your own findings.

Case briefing

This case file follows the criminal proceedings, verdict and sentence recorded in the website case material, alongside subsequent police enquiries, prison-communications questions and the Government’s request to YouTube. The case material records convictions and acquittals returned by a jury, and further police enquiries that had not resulted in further convictions at the time recorded.

Editorial and evidence safeguards

State the jury’s convictions and acquittals exactly. Do not present additional allegations as proven or an appeal as filed unless confirmed by an authoritative record. Protect the lifelong anonymity of the four complainants in the trial. A Government request to a platform is not itself a legal order; attribute platform decisions and official statements.

Case-specific investigation questions

1. Which findings are established by the jury verdict and sentencing remarks, and which allegations were not proven at trial?
2. What do the official police and CPS statements actually confirm about the additional investigations?
3. What further evidence or procedural steps would be needed before any additional allegation could result in a charge or trial?
4. What do the published sentencing remarks say about the sentence, and how do they treat the counts on which the jury acquitted?
5. What questions remain about media organisations, workplace complaints and institutional responses, and which records could answer them?
6. How should public statements and commentary be assessed without treating them as evidence of criminal guilt?
7. What prison rules apply to statements prepared by prisoners and published by family members, and what did the Ministry of Justice investigation ultimately decide?
8. What exactly did the Government ask Google and YouTube to remove, what did the platform say in response, and what content remained available?
9. Has an appeal against conviction or sentence actually been lodged, and if so, what grounds and procedural timetable are recorded by the courts?
10. What is the current status of the two additional rape allegations referred to by the Metropolitan Police, and has the CPS made any charging decision?
11. What evidence supports claims that public authorities or online platforms acted improperly, and how can those claims be distinguished from political opinion or speculation?
12. What legal authority, if any, did the Government rely on when asking Google to remove the video and deactivate the channel, and what remained a voluntary platform decision?
