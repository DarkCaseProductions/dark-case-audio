DARK CASE AUDIO · THE MYSTERY ROOM

The Abduction and Murder of April Jones: The Search Across Mid Wales

Bryn-y-Gog, Machynlleth, Powys, Wales, UK · 1 October 2012

Individual investigator pack

Use this working file to examine the documented record, test explanations and record your own findings.

Case briefing

Five-year-old April Jones disappeared while playing near her home on the Bryn-y-Gog estate in Machynlleth. A major search followed. Mark Bridger was convicted of April’s abduction and murder in 2013 and received a whole-life order. April’s body has never been found.

Editorial and evidence safeguards

Mark Bridger’s conviction is established. The fact that April’s remains have never been recovered does not undo the conviction. Treat search details and trial evidence according to the contemporaneous record.

Case-specific investigation questions

1. What is the most reliable timeline of April’s last known movements?
2. What did witnesses report about the vehicle and disappearance?
3. How did the first police appeals shape the search?
4. What were the scale and challenges of the search in Powys?
5. What forensic and circumstantial evidence was presented at trial?
6. How did the prosecution establish the case without April’s body?
7. What did the jury decide, and what sentence did Bridger receive?
8. Which questions remain about the location of April’s remains?
