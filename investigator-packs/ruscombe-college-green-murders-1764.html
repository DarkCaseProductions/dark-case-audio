DARK CASE AUDIO · THE MYSTERY ROOM

The College Green Murders

College Green, Bristol, England, UK · September 1764

Individual investigator pack

Use this working file to examine the documented record, test explanations and record your own findings.

Case briefing

Frances Ruscombe and her maid Mary Champneys, also recorded as Mary Sweet, were found murdered in their home on College Green, Bristol. The scene suggested robbery, but there was no obvious forced entry and the rear door was reportedly locked from within. No one was prosecuted. A surviving bundle of objects and papers is held at Bristol Archives, catalogue reference TC/Adm/Box/19/1.

Editorial and evidence safeguards

Some details in later accounts remain uncertain, including the exact location of the house and the significance of missing keys. Archive objects may provide clues but do not by themselves solve the murders. Separate contemporary records from later retellings.

Case-specific investigation questions

1. What can the surviving objects and papers tell us about the robbery?
2. How could an intruder have entered and left if the rear door was locked from inside?
3. What do the four missing keys potentially tell us, and what is speculation?
4. What was known about Frances Ruscombe’s household, finances and movements?
5. What do contemporary newspaper reports add, and how should their sensational language be treated?
6. Which suspects were questioned, and why did none face prosecution?
7. What further records might still exist, including the coroner’s inquest papers?
