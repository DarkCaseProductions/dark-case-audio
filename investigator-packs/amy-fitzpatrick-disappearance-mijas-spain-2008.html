Amy Fitzpatrick: The Disappearance on the Costa del Sol

Mijas Costa, Málaga, Spain · 1 January 2008–present

Individual investigator pack

Use this working file to examine the documented record, test explanations and record your own findings.

Case briefing

Amy Fitzpatrick, aged 15, disappeared after leaving a friend’s home in Mijas Costa at approximately 10pm on 1 January 2008. She was expected to walk roughly two kilometres home. Amy has never been found. Her brother Dean’s death in 2013 and David Mahon’s later manslaughter conviction concern a separate case; Mahon has not been charged in connection with Amy’s disappearance.

Editorial and evidence safeguards

Treat this as an unresolved missing-person investigation. Do not imply that any named person was involved without evidence. The manslaughter conviction concerning Dean Fitzpatrick is not evidence about Amy’s disappearance. Attribute family suspicions as opinions, not findings.

Case-specific investigation questions

1. What can be established about Amy’s movements on New Year’s Eve and New Year’s Day before she left Ashley Rose’s home?
2. What was the exact route between the Calypso development and Riviera del Sol, and what were the lighting, terrain and visibility conditions that night?
3. What did Ashley Rose say about the final moments she spent with Amy, and how should that account be assessed alongside contemporaneous reporting?
4. How extensive were the first searches by the Guardia Civil, local police, volunteers and search teams, and which areas or possible routes were examined?
5. What did investigators do with Amy’s mobile phone and computer, and what is independently documented about the collection and examination of digital evidence?
6. What is known about the circumstances of Dean Fitzpatrick’s death and David Mahon’s manslaughter conviction, and why must that separate case not be treated as proof about Amy?
7. Which claims and theories have been raised by different members of Amy’s family, and what evidence, if any, supports each one?
8. What new witness testimony, forensic review or cross-border cooperation could help establish what happened to Amy after 10pm on 1 January 2008?
