The Disappearance of Andrew Gosden: The Journey to London

Doncaster and London, England, UK · 14 September 2007–present

Individual investigator pack

Use this working file to examine the documented record, test explanations and record your own findings.

Case briefing

Andrew Gosden was 14 when he disappeared after travelling alone from Doncaster to London on 14 September 2007. CCTV showed him arriving at King’s Cross and leaving the station at approximately 11.25am. There have been no confirmed sightings since. Two men arrested in December 2021 were eliminated from the investigation in September 2023. The investigation remains open.

Editorial and evidence safeguards

Andrew remains a missing person. Do not declare him dead or state that abduction or trafficking has been proven. Police said the two men arrested in 2021 had no connection to his disappearance. Treat alleged sightings and online theories as unverified unless independently corroborated.

Case-specific investigation questions

1. What is the most reliable minute-by-minute timeline from Andrew leaving home to the last confirmed CCTV sighting at King’s Cross?
2. Why did Andrew return home after initially setting off for school, and what can the available evidence actually establish about that decision?
3. What do the ATM, ticket purchase and railway records establish about his journey, and what do they not tell us?
4. What CCTV coverage existed at King’s Cross in 2007, and which exits or routes were not captured or could not be traced?
5. Were there any credible, independently corroborated sightings after 11.25am on 14 September 2007?
6. What did the December 2021 arrests concern, and why did police later eliminate both men from the investigation?
7. What has South Yorkshire Police said about the current status of the investigation and the information it still needs?
8. Which online claims have been explicitly contradicted or warned against by police, and how can the public avoid spreading harmful misinformation?
