Broadcast with Dark Case Audio

Your podcast. Our radio station. More opportunities to be heard.

Dark Case Audio Radio is inviting independent podcast creators to apply to have their productions considered for our 24/7 internet radio schedule.

We want to help original shows reach new listeners through additional broadcasts and promotion, while creators retain ownership of their work and continue publishing on their existing platforms.

Apply to become a partner

What we're looking for

We welcome established podcasters and new independent creators. We're particularly interested in:

True crime

Investigations, criminal cases and real-life stories.

Paranormal

Hauntings, investigations and unexplained encounters.

Mystery and history

Unsolved events, historical accounts and documentaries.

Horror and storytelling

Original audio drama, fiction and atmospheric storytelling.

Interviews and talk

Well-produced conversations, specialist subjects and discussion.

Other original audio

Have a different kind of show? Tell us about it.

What partnership can offer

  • Consideration for a regular or occasional broadcast slot.
  • Agreed repeat broadcasts at suitable times.
  • Promotion of your show and presenter on Dark Case Audio channels.
  • Links directing listeners to your own website and podcast platforms.
  • An additional place for listeners to discover your work.
There is no application fee. Any broadcast schedule, repeat permissions and promotional arrangements will be agreed with you before an episode is aired. Participation is non-exclusive, and you retain ownership of your production.

What happens next?

  1. Send us your application and a sample episode.
  2. We'll review the programme and get in touch if it is suitable for the station.
  3. If we're interested, we'll discuss the schedule and agree written broadcasting permission before anything is aired.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance or a broadcast slot. We will not broadcast submitted material without your separate agreement.
