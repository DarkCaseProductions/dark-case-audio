Privacy Policy

Dark Case Audio • Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dark Case Productions (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information when you use the Dark Case Audio mobile application, website, membership services, forums and related features (together, the “Services”).

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: your email address and chosen username when you register. Your password is processed by the authentication service used to operate your account; it should be stored in protected, hashed form and is not displayed to us as readable text.
  • Messages and community content: private messages you send or receive, forum posts, and information about blocking or reporting other members where those features are used.
  • Purchase information: details needed to confirm and provide purchases, such as the items purchased, transaction reference and payment status.
  • Technical information: limited information that may be recorded by website, hosting, security or streaming providers when you access the Services, such as IP address, device/browser information, access times and diagnostic logs.

2. How we use information

We use information as necessary to create and manage member accounts, authenticate sign-ins, provide private messaging and forums, apply blocking controls, deliver purchased digital content, operate the radio and other app features, respond to support requests, protect the Services, and meet legal obligations.

3. Payments and Stripe

When you make a purchase, you are directed to a payment page provided by Stripe. Payment card details are entered into and processed by Stripe, not collected directly by the Dark Case Audio app. Stripe may process payment and device information under its own privacy policy. We may receive transaction information such as your purchase, payment status and transaction reference so that we can confirm and fulfil your order.

Stripe privacy information: https://stripe.com/privacy.

4. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or allow access to information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the Services, including providers of website and app hosting, account authentication and database services, payment processing, and radio/audio streaming. These providers process information for the services they supply and under their own terms and privacy arrangements. We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect users, our rights or the security of the Services.

5. Private messages, forums and blocking

Private messages are used to provide member-to-member communication. They are not intended to be public forum posts, but may be processed by the systems that operate the messaging service and may be reviewed where necessary to investigate abuse, respond to a report, protect the Services or comply with law. Blocking another member changes your ability to interact with that member; it does not necessarily erase messages already held by the service. Deleting a message or conversation through the available feature may remove it from your account view, but copies may remain in backups or records where technically necessary or legally required.

Do not include information in messages or forum posts that you do not want the recipient or other permitted viewers to see.

6. Radio, audiobooks and downloads

The Services provide live audio streaming, audiobooks, ebooks and downloadable case-file material. Audio playback and content access may involve our streaming, hosting or content-delivery providers. Listening to or downloading content may generate technical access records with those providers.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The website features opened within the app may use cookies or similar browser storage where needed for sign-in, session management, security and website functionality. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling essential cookies may affect sign-in or other features.

8. Data retention and account deletion

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain your account, fulfil purchases, resolve disputes, protect the Services and meet legal or accounting obligations. You can request account deletion or removal of personal information by emailing darkcaseaudios@gmail.com from your registered email address and including your username. We may need to retain limited information where the law requires it or where it is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Deleting your account may not automatically cancel any separate payment arrangement or remove information already held by a third-party provider; contact that provider where appropriate.

9. Your privacy rights

If you are in the UK, you may have rights under data protection law to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction or objection to certain processing, and data portability where applicable. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

ICO website: https://ico.org.uk/.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website, app, network transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your password confidential and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

11. Children's privacy

The Services contain true-crime and paranormal material that may include disturbing or mature subject matter. They are not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can assess the request and take appropriate action.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Services or our data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.
